Welcome

Posted on July 30, 2010 by savidgereads

Well, however you got here, welcome to The Green Carnation Prize 2010 website. This is the site for all you could need to know about a new prize for works of fiction and memoirs by gay men. Judged by a glittering panel of readers, the Man Fooker Prize is quite probably the first of its kind in the UK. 

Does this sound familiar? Well that’s because originally it started its life as ‘The Man Fooker Prize’ which sadly some people either seemed to find too controversial or possibly a spoof. This prize is neither of those things. It’s about the recognition of gay authors in the literary world today regardless of the genre they write and its one we strongly hope you will support us with under its new title.

Have a wander around the site to find out why the prize was started, just who the heck the judges think they are and what the criteria is for submitting books.

For any additional information you can email us here; greencarnationprize@gmail.com
