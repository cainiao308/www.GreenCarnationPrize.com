Contact Us

Posted on August 1, 2010 by savidgereads

We have made a new page on ‘The Green Carnation Prize’ website so that you can contact us. We shamelessly admit that we want you to get everyone you know joining our facebook group and stalking us on twitter and we feel that between us all we can make it happen. You can find the contact page here in the meantime though here’s our details of how to get hold of us anyway…

via Facebook here in our group (invite your friends)
