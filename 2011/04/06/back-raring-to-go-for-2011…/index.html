Back & Raring To Go For 2011…

Posted on April 6, 2011 by savidgereads

After what was a rather wonderful yet incredibly sudden and quick setting up of The Green Carnation Prize 2010 we have taken a small rest to collect our thoughts and start planning, and indeed plotting, how the prize can be even bigger and better in 2011 (as we hinted back at the end of last year) and now we are back and raring to go.

However the judges for 2011 have already started their roles and are currently swamped in reading material as the submissions are already coming in thick and fast…

In fact already the response to this year’s Green Carnation Prize has been incredible (as we have already had well over ten entries, not all have arrived yet so couldnt be pictured) and we are only just four months in to the year,  so this is really exciting. Over the next few weeks we will be making changes to the website, adding some posts on how the prize will change in 2011 and thoughts from all the judges.

First of all though we would like to hear from all of you! What would you like to see change with the prize in 2011? What would you like to see on The Green Carnation site? Let us know your thoughts on how we can make the site more accessible and interactive with you all and we will see what we can do. Get suggesting, we can’t wait to hear from you all.
