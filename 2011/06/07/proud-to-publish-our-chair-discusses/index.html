Proud To Publish? Our Chair Discusses…

Posted on June 7, 2011 by savidgereads

Well who would have thought it? Only a few days after the relaunch of the site and the Green Carnation not only gets a mention in The Bookseller but its very own post which our chair for 2011, Simon Savidge, was asked to write about the prize and the need for it inside publishing in this day and age. In a piece called ‘Proud To Publish’ he does just that, get ready for the comments, we have a feeling people might have a lot to say on this subject…
