Green Carnation Mention on ‘Books on the Nightstand’…

Posted on June 12, 2011 by savidgereads

We have been thrilled to discover that the Green Carnation Prize has been mentioned on ‘Books on the Nightstand’ which is one of the most popular book podcasts in America. Ann Kingman and Michael Kindness (who are serious book lovers and wonderful hosts of the show) were doing a special show on LGBT authors and which prize then went and got a special mention… That’s right, ours!

You can hear the podcast either on the website here or download it from iTunes.  You can also see a rather interesting list of LGBT books that listeners have recommended. If you have any recommendations then do go and let Ann and Michael know in the comments.

It seems word of the Green Carnation Prize is spreading world-wide which is just what we have been hoping it would.
