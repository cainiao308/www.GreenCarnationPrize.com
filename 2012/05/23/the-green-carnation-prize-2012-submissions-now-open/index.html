The Green Carnation Prize 2012; Submissions Now Open

Posted on May 23, 2012 by savidgereads

After a good few weeks of silence while things have been being sorted behind the scenes we can finally announce that the Green Carnation Prize is back for 2012 as we mentioned previously.

We had intended to relaunch on Monday, however there have been a few hold ups and some exciting new twists and so while everything is sorted once and for all we will have to leave you in suspense for a little longer. It will be worth it though we hope.

We are thrilled to be back and are getting increasingly excited about what the next few months will hold. Expect an official announcement of the judging panel and some other news in the next few days. In the meantime we can officially say that we are open to submissions for The Green Carnation Prize 2012, full details can be found here and the dates for the last submissions to arrive with all five judges is… Friday the 31st of August 2012. We have started receiving several, but always welcome more.
