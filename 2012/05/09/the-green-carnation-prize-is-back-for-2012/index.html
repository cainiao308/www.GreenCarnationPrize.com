The Green Carnation Prize is back for 2012…

Posted on May 9, 2012 by savidgereads

After a good few weeks of silence while things have been being sorted behind the scenes we can finally announce that the Green Carnation Prize is back for 2012. The site will be being updated over the next few days (so bear with it and us) before the prize and the website will be relaunching officially on Monday May the 21st, when the panel of judges and chair of the prize in 2012 will be announced along with some changes to how the prize works and much, much more.

We are thrilled to be back and are getting very excited about what the next few months will hold.

  • About the Prize

    The Green Carnation Prize champions the best writing by LGBTQ authors published in the UK. Named after Oscar Wilde’s emblem, the prize ran from 2010 and partnered with Foyles.
